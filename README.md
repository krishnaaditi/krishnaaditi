## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Wayne State University**, Detroit, Michigan, USA.

My research focuses on **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)** and the reliability of multimodal AI systems.

I am particularly interested in understanding **why, where, and how hallucinations emerge inside MLLMs**. Instead of viewing hallucination only as an incorrect final response, I study the model's **internal multimodal representations** to understand how visual and textual information evolves across layers and how this process eventually leads to either grounded or hallucinated outputs.

My work explores ways to **detect hallucination, identify its internal causes, localize hallucination-sensitive representations, and mitigate hallucination through targeted interventions**.

My broader goal is to develop **grounded, interpretable, reliable, and trustworthy multimodal AI systems**.

<b>Why do Multimodal Large Language Models hallucinate?</b>
