I am a **Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Wayne State University**, Detroit, Michigan, USA. My research focuses on **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**, with an emphasis on understanding **why, where, and how hallucinations emerge** inside multimodal models.

My research explores the **causes of hallucination, internal multimodal representations, hallucination detection and mitigation, and vision-language alignment**, with the broader goal of developing **more grounded, reliable, and trustworthy multimodal AI systems**.

#### I aim to understand not only when multimodal models hallucinate, but why they hallucinate — and how we can make them more trustworthy.
